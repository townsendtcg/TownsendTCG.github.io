Townsend TCG consignment terms (v1, Oct 10, 2026)

1. Your cards. You own these cards, they're authentic, they aren't stolen, and you have the right to sell them. You're 18 or older.

2. What I do. Townsend TCG (Austin Townsend) sells the cards for you on eBay, TCGplayer, Whatnot or at card shows. I grade condition using TCGplayer's condition guide and I set the asking price. I will never sell a card for less than the bottom price you set. If I think a card is fake, altered or not as described, I'll hand it back and won't list it.

3. Fees. My fee is 15% of the sale price. The selling platform's fees (eBay, TCGplayer, Whatnot or card processing) and the shipping label also come out of the sale. You get the rest. Example: a card that sells for $100 at a show pays you $85. The same card sold on eBay pays you about $66 after the 15%, eBay's fees and the label.

4. Payment. I pay you 7 days after the buyer receives the card (7 days after the sale for show sales), using the payout method you give here. If a buyer returns the card, opens a claim or reverses the payment, that sale is undone: I'll hold your payout until it's settled, and if you were already paid, the amount comes out of your next payout or you pay it back.

5. How long. I hold your cards for 90 days. Anything unsold after 90 days goes back to you unless you choose to renew. You can pick up at one of my shows, or I'll mail them back with tracking and you pay the postage. You can ask for any unsold card back at any time.

6. Care. Your cards are kept in sleeves and toploaders and handled carefully. If a card is lost or damaged while I have it because of my own mistake, I'll pay you your bottom price for it. I'm not responsible for loss or damage I can't control, like theft, fire or natural disaster, or for a buyer's claim that the platform decides against the seller after I've shipped as described.

7. Your info. Your contact and payout details and your photos are used only to run your consignment. I don't sell or share them.

8. The rest. Either of us can end this for unsold cards at any time. These terms are governed by Florida law.
