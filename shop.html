How requests work

Requesting a card doesn't charge you anything. It just lets Austin know you want it and how to reach you. He'll confirm it's still available and send payment details (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Zelle or JIM), the same as at a show, then ship once paid.

This list is updated by hand after shows, so it can lag what's actually in the case by a day or two. Looking for something that isn't here? Send a wish list and Austin will check the binders.
