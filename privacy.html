Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

Townsend TCG is a small Pokémon card business based in Saint Augustine, Florida. This policy covers our website and the posting tool we use to share our own videos to TikTok and Instagram.

What the posting tool does

The tool is used only by Townsend TCG to publish videos we made to our own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

Information we access

When we connect our TikTok account, the tool receives our basic TikTok profile info (display name and account ID) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account. It does not access anyone else's account, messages, contacts, or viewing history.

How it's stored

Login tokens are held by our integration provider, Composio, so the tool can post on our behalf. Videos come from our own Google Drive. We do not sell, rent, or share any data with advertisers.

Visitors to this website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't run our own analytics or ads here. GitHub may log standard technical data like IP addresses, as described in its own privacy policy.

Removing access

We can disconnect the tool from TikTok at any time from TikTok's app settings or from our integration dashboard, which deletes the stored login token.

Contact

Questions? Send us a DM on Instagram at @Townsend_TCG.
